A SMALL COLLECTION

Field notes.

A few thoughts about everyday things, and the pleasure of paying attention.

Leave a little room

A clear desk does not have to be an empty desk. A notebook, a good lamp, and enough space to put down a cup can be a useful beginning.

The same idea works for a day: a little unscheduled time leaves room to notice something unexpected.

Take the longer walk

A familiar route changes with the weather and the light. Taking a slower turn through a quiet street can make an ordinary errand feel different.

There is no need to record every detail. Sometimes it is enough to look.

Keep what is useful

A small collection of well-used things often has more character than a shelf of things kept for later. Care, repair, and a little patience can go a long way.
